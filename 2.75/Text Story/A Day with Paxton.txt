===== A Day with Paxton =====
Narration: Paxton invited Player to have a Pokémon battle.
Narration: They kept it up, lost in the thrill of battle, until night fell...
Paxton: Buckle up! We’re not done yet!
Paxton: Let’s go, Player! Keep this energy going!
Paxton: On second thought, maybe this is a good time to pack it in.
Paxton: Part of being a Trainer is knowing when to give it a rest and take care of yourself!
Player: Aren’t YOU tired, Paxton?
Paxton: Nah, Emboar and I are fine.
Player: Yeah, I’m worn out...
Paxton: Sorry! I should’ve noticed, but our battle was just so exciting!
Paxton: Y’know, in Lumiose City, the Z-A Royale is held at night.
Paxton: So I’d be getting into gear right about now.
Paxton: In Lumiose, every day was a challenge to overcome our limits!
Paxton: The Z-A Royale is a race against time.
Paxton: You have to try to take down Trainers and rack up points as fast as you can.
Paxton: I tried to outdo myself each night.
Paxton: I wanted to keep breaking my previous record!
Paxton: I tried to stay cool and come up with reasonable, detailed ways to win...
Paxton: but no matter how calmly I planned, I couldn’t help burning with excitement!
Paxton: I bet you felt the same way, huh, Emboar.
Paxton: Every time we entered battle, you were sizzling with energy.
Paxton: When Emboar Mega Evolves, his temperature ratchets up even more.
Paxton: Every attack he makes blazes with intensity!
Paxton: That’s what makes Emboar a good match for me. Battling brings out the fire in us.
Paxton: Whenever we’re battling, we’re having one heck of a time!
Paxton: Don’t get me wrong. Emboar’s not the type to battle whoever’s in sight.
Paxton: But if he sees an ally in trouble, he’ll rush in and start fighting for ’em on the spot. That’s how passionate he is!
Paxton: Same goes for me. If somebody or their Pokémon needs help, no matter where, I’ll come running.
Paxton: Emboar and I are similar like that!
