===== A Day with Sygna Suit Calem =====
Narration: Player came across Calem and Gloria in the midst of battle.
Sygna Suit Calem: You watched our battle just now, right?
Sygna Suit Calem: We pitted Dynamax against Mega Evolution with our fire Pokémon.
Gloria: Our partner Pokémon have another thing in common!
Sygna Suit Calem: Oh yeah! Turns out we raised both from Eggs we got from friends.
Gloria: I got Scorbunny’s Egg from Hop...
Sygna Suit Calem: And I raised Fennekin into Delphox from an Egg Serena gave me.
Sygna Suit Calem: The strong bond we formed made Mega Evolution possible.
Sygna Suit Calem: It’s given me more confidence than ever!
Sygna Suit Calem: I want to show Serena my thanks for bringing Delphox and I together...
Sygna Suit Calem: so I was thinking someday, I’d like Delphox and I to battle her.
Gloria: The way Delphox uses fire is just like magic! It surprised me!
Gloria: I bet Serena would also be surprised to see how strong he’s gotten!
Gloria: I was even more amazed to learn that when he Mega Evolves, he can fly around on a branch!
Sygna Suit Calem: Well, confusing opponents is Mega-Evolved Delphox’s specialty.
Sygna Suit Calem: I’ve been learning what attacks are possible by watching him.
Player: You’ve learned so much!
Sygna Suit Calem: Yeah. When I’m with Delphox, each day is filled with discoveries.
Player: Maybe you and Delphox are alike!
Sygna Suit Calem: As far as our battle styles go, not really.
Gloria: Y’know, both Cinderace and I love to eat.
Gloria: Are you sure there’s nothing you and Delphox have in common?
Sygna Suit Calem: Hmm...
Sygna Suit Calem: He may have more in common with Serena.
Sygna Suit Calem: He likes it whenever I trim his fur or brush him...
Sygna Suit Calem: and Serena’s more the type to care about her appearance.
Sygna Suit Calem: But, hmm...
Sygna Suit Calem: I think we should value people and Pokémon’s differences.
Sygna Suit Calem: Even so, finding common ground may make it easier to strengthen your bond as a sync pair.
Sygna Suit Calem: I hope I can keep spending time with Delphox...
Sygna Suit Calem: and learn even more about him—both things we have in common and things we don’t!
