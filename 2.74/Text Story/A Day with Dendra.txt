===== A Day with Dendra =====
Narration: Player was accompanying Dendra on a run across Pasio.
Dendra: Phew! That was a good workout!
Dendra: You’ve got a good pair of legs on ya, Player!
Dendra: I couldn’t help getting fired up over how good it felt to run...
Dendra: but are you OK keeping this pace?
Player: I’m still good!
Dendra: I like your energy!
Dendra: But I can tell you’re pushing yourself too hard. You can’t fool a teacher!
Player: I’m a little tired...
Dendra: I thought so... Sorry!
Dendra: Let’s pick this up another time!
Dendra: Give me a shout anytime you want to build stamina!
Dendra: Sounds like Medicham will support us again!
Dendra: She always keeps me company when I’m strength training or out on a run.
Dendra: Medicham have honed spiritual power...
Dendra: so they make good partners who’ll cheer you on when you’re exercising!
Dendra: Of course, I also rely on mine in Pokémon battles!
Dendra: Medicham’s advantage against Psychic-type Pokémon is greater than other Fighting types’!
Dendra: After all, Medicham is both Fighting type and Psychic type.
Dendra: There aren’t many matchups that’d put her at a disadvantage. I don’t have to worry about sending her out!
Dendra: But...
Dendra: Even when our weaknesses are covered, some opponents are still too strong.
Dendra: Whoops! Can’t go moping around!
Dendra: As a battle studies teacher, I gotta get so strong, I lose to no one!
Dendra: Isn’t that right, Medicham?!
Dendra: I’ve a ways to go! I’ve got to hone my spirit just like Medicham!
Dendra: Player! Let’s do lots of training and battling together!
Dendra: We’ll train ourselves inside and out for a strong mind and a strong body!
