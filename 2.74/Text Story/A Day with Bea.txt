===== A Day with Bea =====
Narration: While walking through town, Player ran into Bea and Machamp.
Bea (Alt.): Oh, Player. Fancy seeing you here.
Bea (Alt.): I just reached a good stopping point for today.
Player: What were you doing?
Bea (Alt.): I was doing some stretches on my way home from Pasio Academy.
Player: Heading home from school?
Bea (Alt.): Yes. Since I finished my classes, I was just doing some stretches.
Bea (Alt.): When I’m not on duty as Gym Leader, I try to loosen up.
Bea (Alt.): I recently made it a habit to help calm myself down.
Bea (Alt.): And these clothes actually make it easier to relax.
Bea (Alt.): When I’m in my Gym uniform, I can’t help but think of battling.
Bea (Alt.): In fact... Once, when I was pushing myself too hard, Machamp stepped in to stop me.
Bea (Alt.): Ever since, I’ve seen rest as another aspect of training.
Bea (Alt.): We’ll need it if Machamp and I want to defeat Leon.
Player: That sounds like a good change!
Bea (Alt.): I agree. I feel like my perspective’s broadened.
Player: I think that’s important!
Bea (Alt.): Yes. I’m glad I finally noticed.
Bea (Alt.): Also, after visiting Pasio’s dessert shops with you, Player...
Bea (Alt.): I think I’ve changed how I see strength.
Bea (Alt.): I understand now. Time spent training and battling isn’t the only thing that makes one stronger.
Bea (Alt.): Oh...that reminds me.
Bea (Alt.): I bought some sweets at a shop Nessa told me about.
Bea (Alt.): I like having something sugary on hand when I study or train.
Bea (Alt.): Would you like to eat some together, Player?
Bea (Alt.): Of course, sweets are delicious even when you’re alone...
Bea (Alt.): But they taste much better with company!
