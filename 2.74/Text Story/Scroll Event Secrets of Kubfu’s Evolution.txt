===== What Kind of Pokémon Is Urshifu? =====
Avery: Victor... I’ve heard it’s about time for this Kubfu to evolve.
Klara: Doesn’t it become a Pokémon called, like, Urshifu?
Victor (Academy): That’s right!
Victor (Academy): We’ll finally be able to show off the results of our training with Bea’s lot!
Avery: According to my psychic powers, Urshifu’s strikes flow like a river, raining down nonstop on opponents.
Klara: Pfft! Unfortunately for you, that’s dead wrong!
Klara: The master told me Urshifu loves going for a one-hit KO with a single devastating blow.
Avery: Nope! My information also came from the master!
Avery: Perhaps YOU misheard things?
Klara: Huh?! No way!
Victor (Academy): Settle down, you two.
Victor (Academy): Neither of you is wrong!
Klara: Huh?
Avery: Hm? So that means...
Avery: Urshifu rains down one-hit KOs?! Impossible!
Victor (Academy): No, no! That’s not it.
Victor (Academy): Urshifu has two different forms!
Klara: Wha—?!
Avery: Say what?!
Victor (Academy): There’s Single Strike Style, which prefers heavy, individual hits...
Victor (Academy): and there’s Rapid Strike Style, which excels at flowing multi-hits!
Klara: I see! I’m curious what form you’ll evolve him into, Victor.
Avery: Indeed! I must know, so that I may win our future battles!
Victor (Academy): I was gonna head to the academy to pick up two types of scrolls. Kubfu needs one to evolve.
Victor (Academy): Would you two like to come watch him evolve?
Klara: Can we really?!
Avery: In that case, I’ll get a head start and meet you there!
Avery: Avery, Teleport!
Klara: You’re just sprinting away again!
